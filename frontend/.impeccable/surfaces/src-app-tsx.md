# Surface brief: GPR site (Головна · Патерни · Визначити)

Scope: whole frontend. Home page is Persuade; Detect and Patterns pages are Operate inside the same world.
Audience: beginner guitarists, phone and laptop equally (PRODUCT.md). Job: understand what GPR does in one viewport, then upload a song.
User steer (round 2): liked Ватра's colours and vibe; home page must be bigger and animated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A campfire stage at night: the song's waveform glows like embers and settles, beat by beat, into the strum pattern you play. Refuses the cool-navy SaaS hero with a centred headline over a row of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm night ground #171310, coal surfaces #2a221c, ember #ff7a1a for the primary action and downstrokes, amber #ffb347 for upstrokes and highlights, smoke #f4ead9 for text. Rubik heavy rounded display, Golos Text body. Flat colour, glow only on the animated sound.

STORY: The visitor sees a song turn into arrows, understands "it listens and tells me how to strum", reads how and why in three plain steps, glances at the six patterns, and uploads a track from the hero or the closing band.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav top (Головна · Патерни · ember pill «Визначити патерн»). Left 45%: headline «Почуй пісню — отримай бій», one-line lead, working drop zone with ember button. Right 55%: the stage, three stacked lanes (Пісня waveform, Гітара isolated bars, Як грати 8 beat cells) animating in sequence.

FORM: grounded candidate 2 of 7 (Ватра), chosen in the safer re-roll; seed:2b0ba1b9f650672d. Signature interaction: the pipeline loop, waveform → guitar stem → beat cells lighting with arrows; the How-it-works section replays each stage as it scrolls into view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
